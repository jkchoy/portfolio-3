If heaven had a creek, it would be this one

Schitt's Creek

This project consisted of three assignments focused on graphic typography. First, I created original typographic artwork and applied it to two product mock-ups: a soda can and a record album cover. The third component involved designing graphic typography based on a selected quote. The overall goal was to explore how typography can visually express mood and convey meaning across different formats.

Client: Schitt's Creek

Project Type: Graphic Typography

Competencies: Photography, Typography, Layout, Composition

Software: InDesign, Photoshop, Illustrator

Exploring multiple ideas before defining the solution

brainstorming process


Once I selected Schitt's Creek as my overarching theme, I focused on defining a clear approach for each project. I wanted to capture the essence of the Schitt family—over-the-top, dramatic, yet elegant and dynamic—while highlighting individual characters and the show as a whole. To achieve this, I decided to use typeface as a strong visual element and a minimal color palette, allowing the personality of the characters and the show's tone to take center stage in each design.

for alexis' cover i stubbled across the typeface Citrus Gothic Rough and instantly knew it was perfect—for its name and Citrus Gothic Rough brings a slightly weathered, textured edge to a classic gothic structure. Its roughened finish feels effortlessly cool.

For Moira's Rosé can, I chose Kepler Std—an elegant serif with subtle calligraphic details that give it a refined yet expressive feel . Its theatrical, old-world quality suits Moira Rose perfectly. I kept the design minimal, letting the typeface carry the style.

For David's quote, I used Didot—the signature typeface of Schitt's Creek—paired with a simple cursive accent to add a softer, more expressive touch. The black rose border is inspired by the shirt David wore in that iconic scene, tying the design back to his distinct style.
