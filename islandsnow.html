Island Snow

In this project, my task was to select a well-established Hawaii-based local brand with over 25 years of history and animate its logo in a way that reflected the brand's identity, honored its heritage, and strengthened its visual storytelling. To ensure the logo remained sharp and animation-ready, I recreated it in Adobe Illustrator, as the original file was too pixelated. I manually redrew the logo and used Auto Trace on the business name to convert it into editable vector paths, which allowed me to create a clean 3D effect for the text.

Client: Island Snow

Project Type: Logo Animation

Competencies: After Effects, Illustrator

Software: Animation, Visual Style, Audio
