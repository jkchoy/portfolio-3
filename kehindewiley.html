Typography Layout

If heaven had a creek, it would be this one

KEHINDE WILEY

I was tasked with designing magazine layouts that explored the relationship between typography, color, layout structure, grid systems, and white space, allowing the content to guide the overall visual style and composition. Featuring the work of Kehinde Wiley, whose art is bold and highly detailed, required a careful approach to avoid cropping, distortion, or visual competition with the artwork. To support his work without distracting from it, I focused on clean white space, thoughtful image placement, and supportive typography, ensuring the layouts remained visually engaging while presenting the information clearly and keeping the artwork as the focal point.

Client: Nicole Martinez

Project Type: Editorial Design

Competencies: Photography, Typography, Layout, Composition

Software: InDesign, Photoshop

Exploring multiple ideas before defining the solution

braindstroming process


Once I selected Schitt's Creek as my overarching theme, I focused on defining a clear approach for each project. I wanted to capture the essence of the Schitt family—over-the-top, dramatic, yet elegant and dynamic—while highlighting individual characters and the show as a whole. To achieve this, I decided to use typeface as a strong visual element and a minimal color palette, allowing the personality of the characters and the show's tone to take center stage in each design.
