About Me

Hi, I'm Jackie.

I've found passion in two worlds that might seem different at first glance—massage therapy and interface design.


I'm a Cosmetologist and Massage Therapist. I've been running my own private practice since April 2017, JKC Wellness. But I'm also a student at KCC's New Media Arts studying Interface Design, earning credits to (hopefully!) make my way into acupuncture school. Because why stop at helping you relax when I could also learn to balance your qi?


As a massage therapist, I help people reconnect with their bodies and well-being. As a graphic designer and UX/UI coder, I bring ideas to life through intuitive, beautiful digital experiences. Both allow me to create, connect, and make a positive impact.


I'm a mom to two amazing humans (18 & 12). When I'm not being mom, a business owner, and a college student, you'll probably find me breaking a sweat at Fit for a Goddess or boxing.


I'm a lifetime student.


Welcome to my structured chaotic life!
